## 📌 Project Overview
FreshBite is a responsive, frontend-only online food ordering web application designed with a warm, food-focused UI (`#FF6B35` Warm Orange, `#FFF8F0` Cream, `#2D1B12` Dark Brown). Users can browse restaurants, search and filter dishes by category and dietary preferences, inspect nutritional details, manage their shopping cart, apply coupon codes, complete simulated checkout, and track orders in real time.

---

## ✨ Key Features Implemented
- **Home & Special Offers:** Hero banner, promotional coupon codes (`FRESH20`, `WELCOME50`), and quick category navigation.
- **Restaurant Listing & Filters:** Filter restaurants by cuisine, customer rating, and price tier.
- **Interactive Food Menu:** Real-time search bar, category filters, and dietary labels/filters (**Veg**, **Non-Veg**, **Vegan**).
- **Product Details Modal:** View full dish description, ingredients, nutritional breakdown, and verified reviews.
- **Shopping Cart & Billing:** Add/remove items, `+`/`-` quantity controls, automatic subtotal, free delivery threshold (`₹299+`), and coupon discounts saved via `localStorage`.
- **Checkout & Simulated Payment:** Delivery address input, delivery slot selection, form validation, and simulated payment confirmation.
- **User Account & Live Order Tracking:** Simulated login/register, saved addresses, wishlist (`❤️`), order history, and step-by-step live order tracking.
- **Responsive Design:** Mobile-friendly layout with hamburger navigation (`☰`) across desktop, tablet, and mobile viewports.
